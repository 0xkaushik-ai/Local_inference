  1. Local AI. Ready for your app? Your hardware. Your model. A few important questions.
  2. Meet DeviceBench. Three tools. One local workspace.
  3. Local AI Doctor. Check your runtime, installed models, and hardware. These checks are read-only and do not load a model.
  4. Model & Context Checker. Inspect context limits and estimate memory. Confirm actual loading with your runtime.
  5. App Compatibility Tester. Test streaming, structured JSON, tool calling, or embeddings. Support depends on the selected model and server.
  6. Start with what you are building. Choose a preset for a chatbot, data extraction, tool assistant, or semantic search. Presets select API checks; they do not validate an entire application.
  7. Keep the evidence. Download a readable HTML report or take the JSON into your workflow.
  8. Your machine. Clear next steps. Diagnose, inspect, test, and export. No account required.
  9. Explore the V1 preview. DeviceBench is available on GitHub.
